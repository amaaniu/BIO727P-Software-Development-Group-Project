Directed Evolution Monitoring & Analysis

Provides monitoring and analytics tools for researchers participating in directed evolution experiments, for faster and more informed decision-making. Track evolutionary trajectories, variant libraries, and mutations across cycles. Free access for all.

Platform Features

Upload your experimental data in JSON or TSV format and use the following features to analyse your results:

Plasmid Validation

Validate that the desired wild-type protein is encoded in the plasmid FASTA data

Activity Score Calculation

Calculates and records the activity score of each variant

Performance Comparison

Compare performance across different generations

Variant Visualization

Highlights the top performing variants

Mutation Tracking

Track mutations over generations

Comprehensive Reports

Generate and export comprehensive reports on your study
