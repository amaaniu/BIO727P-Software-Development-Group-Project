Welcome to the Directed Evolution Web Monitoring Portal

This portal provides monitoring and analytics for researchers participating in directed evolution experiments.

Upload your experimental data in JSON or TSV format here and use the following features to analyse your results:

  • Validate that the desired wild-type protein is encoded in the plasmid FASTA data
  • Calculates and records the activity score of each variant
  • Compare performance across different generations
  • Visualise the top performing variants
  • Track mutations over generations
  • Generate comprehensive reports on your study

To get started and upload your experimental data, please register for an account or log in if you already have an account.
